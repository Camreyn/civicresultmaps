# Wisconsin 2024 Electronic Integrity Records Request

This request seeks machine-readable records that can reconcile electronic election-system output against official results, paper/audit evidence, and custody records. Existing advisory indicators are not allegations or proof of tampering.

- Cast vote records: status=partial; grain=county_availability_scaffold; reconciliation=availability_scaffold_only_no_cvr_loaded. Suggested custodian/path: Wisconsin Elections Commission, county clerks, or municipal clerks depending on record custody.

- Tabulator and EMS logs: status=needs_data; grain=tabulator_or_ems; reconciliation=not_inventoried. Suggested custodian/path: Wisconsin Elections Commission, county clerks, or municipal clerks depending on record custody.

- Audit results: status=partial; grain=selected_reporting_unit; reconciliation=selected_units_loaded_per_unit_outcomes_not_published. Suggested custodian/path: Wisconsin Elections Commission, county clerks, or municipal clerks depending on record custody.
- Chain of custody: status=needs_data; grain=ballot_equipment_media; reconciliation=not_inventoried. Suggested custodian/path: Wisconsin Elections Commission, county clerks, or municipal clerks depending on record custody.

Preferred production format: original exports where available, CSV, XLSX, JSON, log bundles, audit workpapers, or record layouts/data dictionaries. Please preserve original filenames, timestamps, export settings, and field definitions.

If your office does not maintain a requested record, please identify the state, county, municipal, vendor, or other custodian most likely to maintain it. If fees are expected, please provide an estimate before processing.

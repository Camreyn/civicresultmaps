Political Groups:

C – Alaska Constitution Party

Other:

N – Nonpartisan (no party affiliation)
U – Undeclared (no party declared)

Political Parties are those parties that have gained recognized political party status under Alaska Statutes 15.60.010(25) or by court order. 

Political Groups are those groups that have applied for party status but have not met the qualifications to be a recognized political party under Alaska Statutes 15.60.010(24).

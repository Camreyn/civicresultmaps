MultiTech Lifecycle Management Stages Definitions

NRND

Not Recommended for New Designs

  • Devices are actively being produced and sold
  • MultiTech anticipates that an End-ofLife (EOL) notification is approaching
  • Replacement models have been identified and should be considered for new solution designs

NEOL

Near-End-of-Life (Last Time buy)

  • An End-of-Life (EOL) plan is in place for this device
  • Devices are actively being produced and sold
  • Quantity limitations may apply due to allocations made on a first-come, first-served basis
  • Place any Last-Time-Buy (LTB) orders
  • Begin evaluating recommended replacements

EOL

End-of-Life

  • Devices are no longer being produced or sold by MultiTech
  • EOL devices will continue to have warranty coverage through the standard warranty period based on the original purchase date
